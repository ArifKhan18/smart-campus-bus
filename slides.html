Tracking System

A real-time GPS-based bus tracking & campus transit management web application

Supervisor: Humayra Ahmed, Assistant Professor Intake 51 | Section 3 Md Arif Khan  •  Karnia Binte Rafique  •  Suraiya Karim  •  Prosenjit Biswas  •  Proshanta Saha

Problem Statement & Project Objectives

02

Addressing campus transit inefficiencies through an automated, real-time tracking and communication ecosystem.

❌ Existing Challenges

  • 1. Blind Waiting: No live bus location or arrival ETA at stops
  • 2. Unreliable Schedules: Paper notices with unexpected delays
  • 3. Communication Gap: No passenger-driver coordination channel
  • 4. Administrative Blindness: Zero real-time fleet overview or reports

🎯 Target Objectives & Solutions

  • ✓ Real-Time GPS Tracking: High-precision location updates every 5s
  • ✓ Haversine ETA Engine: Accurate stop-wise distance & arrival times
  • ✓ Live Community Chat: Bus-specific instant communication
  • ✓ Fleet Management Portal: Interactive route builder & analytics

Development Methodology & Workflow

04

Iterative Agile development process emphasizing high reliability, real-time synchronization, and modularity.

📋 Agile Phases (1 — 3)

Phase 1: Architecture & NoSQL Schema: Designed normalized User, Bus, Route, and Notice models in Firestore with indexed queries.

Phase 2: Responsive Prototyping: Built mobile-first interfaces with full multi-theme support (Classic Navy, Terracotta, Oceanic).

Phase 3: Secure REST API: Implemented ASP.NET Core endpoints with Brevo SMTP 6-digit OTP verification and JWT claims.

🚀 Agile Phases (4 — 6)

Phase 4: GPS Tracking & ETA Calculation: Integrated HTML5 Geolocation API with Leaflet and Haversine distance engine.

Phase 5: Real-time Messaging & Broadcast: Built bus-wise community group chats and urgent notice board.

Phase 6: Verification & Testing: Executed xUnit backend unit tests and Playwright E2E integration test suites.

Key Differentiators & Measurable Impact

10

SmartBus combines mathematical precision with seamless real-time interaction to deliver a superior campus commute.

✨ Key Differentiators

  • 🧮 Haversine Formula ETA: Mathematical calculation based on geographical coordinates
  • 💬 Bus-Wise Chatrooms: Real-time Firestore communication per active bus
  • 🌐 Bilingual Driver UI: Full English & বাংলা localization
  • 🗺️ Visual Route Builder: Click-to-place stops directly on the map

📈 Measurable Impact

  • ⚡ Zero Blind Waiting: Students track bus locations before heading to stops
  • 📢 Instant Delay Alerts: Push notices reduce campus confusion during delays
  • 🛡️ Enhanced Student Safety: Direct reporting pipeline with admin response
  • 📊 Data-Driven Transit: Analytics help optimize bus schedules and fleet size

Business Model & Monetization Strategy

11

A high-margin B2B SaaS transit management platform converting university bus fleets into a recurring subscription business.

🎯 Target Market

  • 🏫 150+ Universities: Public & Private campuses (BUBT, NSU, BRAC)
  • 🎒 K-12 Schools: Child safety & parental live tracking
  • 🏢 Corporate Fleets: IT & industrial employee shuttles

💎 Value Proposition

  • 📱 Zero Hardware Cost: 100% Smartphone GPS (No GPS box needed)
  • ⏱️ Zero Blind Waiting: Real-time ETA & live delay alerts
  • ⛽ 20-30% Savings: Fleet route & schedule optimization

💰 Revenue Streams

  • 🔄 B2B SaaS: $15 - $25 (৳1,500 - ৳2,500) / bus / month
